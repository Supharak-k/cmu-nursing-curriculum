3Applicant Qualifications

Academic Requirements

  • Applicants must have graduated from or be in the final term of Matthayom Suksa 6 (M.6) in the Thailand secondary education system (Native Thais are excluded from this category).
  • Applicants may graduate from an international school in Thailand with a high school certificate or equivalent.
  • Applicants may graduate from an overseas institution or hold a certificate listed in the secondary education equivalency system, excluding BTEC and other vocational school diplomas or higher education credentials.

Note: Native Thais who graduated from or are currently studying in M.6 within the Thailand secondary education system are not eligible for this program.

CGPA Requirements

  • Thai System: A minimum overall CGPA of 3.0 is required, and the applicant must have Chemistry and Biology in their transcript or listed in a standardized test, such as AP exam scores.
  • Equivalency Certification: No minimum overall grade is required, but Chemistry and Biology must be present in the transcript or listed in a standardized test.

English Proficiency Requirements

Applicants must submit one of the following scores:

  • IELTS5.5
  • TOEFL PBT/ITP525
  • TOEFL CBT196
  • TOEFL iBT70
  • CMU IPET60
  • Duolingo DET95

If taking the CMU English Placement Test, applicants must score at least 60% of the total score.

Health Qualifications

  • Applicants must be in good health without illnesses, symptoms, or disabilities that would obstruct their studies.
  • Ineligible conditions include severe psychiatric problems, contagious diseases in the transmission phase, and severe non-communicable diseases such as uncontrolled epilepsy, severe heart disease, severe hypertension, chronic kidney disease, or substance use disorder.
  • Applicants are ineligible if they have visual problems, including severe color blindness, or visual acuity in their good eye of less than 6/12 or 20/40 after correction.
  • Applicants are ineligible if they have a sensory neural hearing loss with a threshold above 40 dB at 500-2,000 Hz.

4Required Application Documents

General Documents

An official graduation certificate (or current student status certificate), official academic transcript, English proficiency test results, personal identification (Thai National ID or Passport), and a recent photograph in a uniform or formal attire.

Program-Specific Documents

A health certificate confirming good health with no visual problems, including severe blindness.

Applicants must self-certify in the online application system that they do not have any illnesses, symptoms, or disabilities that would be an obstacle to their studies.
